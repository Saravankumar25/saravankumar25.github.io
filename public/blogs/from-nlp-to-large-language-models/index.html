How to use this blog

Read the chapters in order: each one introduces its own vocabulary, explains the intuition first, then shows the math or mechanism, and finishes with the trade-offs that matter in practice. Equations always come with a plain-English reading of every symbol, so you can skip the math on a first pass and return to it later.

  • Chapters 1–2 build the foundations: text, tokens, embeddings, recurrent networks, attention and the Transformer.
  • Chapters 3–4 open the Transformer up: modern attention variants, positional encodings, normalization, and encoder-only models such as BERT.
  • Chapters 5–7 turn the architecture into LLMs: generation, scaling, efficient inference and training, finetuning and alignment.
  • Chapters 8–10 cover reasoning models, retrieval and agents, evaluation, and where the field is heading.
