TDM Guide

Test Data Management from absolute zero to hands-on tool work: foundations and ETL first, then Informatica TDM, GenRocket and Delphix, with worked examples, labs and a capstone project.

How this guide was researched

Delphix and GenRocket content comes from their official help sites, downloaded in full; screenshots are linked from those pages. Informatica content comes from the TDM 10.5.10 User Guide PDF, and its figures are cited by page number. The three YouTube videos could not be transcribed, so none of their content is used. Details and every limitation are on the Sources & limitations page.

How to use this guide

  1. Read Chapters 1 to 3 in order.

    They assume nothing. Every term is defined the first time it appears, and the glossary at the end of Chapter 10 collects them.

  2. Take the tool chapters as the curriculum reaches them.

    Informatica (Days 43 to 45 and 54), GenRocket (Days 45 to 50), Delphix (Days 51 to 56). Each section ends with a recap.

  3. Do the labs, then the capstone.

    Concepts stick when you do them yourself. Chapter 10 ties all three tools into the project case study.

The ten chapters

CHAPTER 01

Welcome: why TDM exists, the data it handles and the rules around it

CHAPTER 02

The core techniques: discover, subset, mask and generate

CHAPTER 03

ETL from scratch, and how it meets TDM

Extract, transform, load explained step by step, then how ETL and TDM overlap, differ and work together in real projects.

CHAPTER 04

Informatica TDM (1): architecture, the tool tour, discovery and subsetting

CHAPTER 06

GenRocket (2): data patterns and a hands-on build

Delphix (2): algorithms, file masking and hands-on labs

CHAPTER 10

Putting it together: real engagements, the capstone and the glossary
