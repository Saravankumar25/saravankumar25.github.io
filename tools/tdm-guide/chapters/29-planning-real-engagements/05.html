29.15 KPIs: how you show it worked

Measure before you start, or you can only say "it feels better". The four KPIs below are a common starting set. Capture the baseline in the first phase of the plan.

29.19 Recap

Recap
  • Every engagement follows one life cycle: use cases, landscape, sensitivity, tool choice, POC, assessment, deployment, sign-off. Generation and masking differ in emphasis, not order.
  • Find use cases by interviewing; record them in a register with a type and priority.
  • Inventory the landscape and rate each column; check every row against what the tools can read.
  • The three tools have different centres of gravity: Informatica TDM is one console for discover, subset, mask and generate; GenRocket is synthetic generation, with G-Subset for subsetting and masking; Delphix pairs masking with virtual copies. Gaps in the documentation mean "not covered", not "not possible".
  • A POC needs measurable criteria, a copy, and a pass or fail decision made in advance.
  • The assessment, risk register, deployment plan and sign-off checklist turn the POC into a service.
  • Keep keys and seeds under change control, keep audit logs, define RACI and KPIs.
