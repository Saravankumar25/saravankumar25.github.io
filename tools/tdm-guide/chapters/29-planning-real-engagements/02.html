The same SSN appears in L1, L3 and L4. That is the central integrity requirement: whatever masks it must give the same result everywhere, or the partner's test data no longer joins to ShopSphere's. The masking concepts are in the masking concepts chapter.

29.6 Sensitivity assessment

For each column in scope, decide whether it is sensitive, how badly, and what to do. Do not trust column names alone: both Informatica and Delphix profile the data itself as well as the names. Informatica discovers data domains by patterns in data or metadata (User Guide 10.5.10, p.31). Delphix's ASDD profiler adds data-level classifiers, which found fields called DATA_00 and DATA_01 as phone and email in an official screenshot (see the Delphix profiling chapter). A simple, defensible rating is impact if leaked (1 to 3) times exposure in test (1 to 3). General concept
